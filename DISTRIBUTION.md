It does not require publishing modified source code. The copyright and permission notice must remain with copies or substantial portions of the software.

This package includes the full original MIT notice, plus bundled third-party notices. The installed extension includes them in `licenses/`, so distributing only the extension still distributes its required notices. Do not remove them when preparing an upload.

For a public Chrome Web Store release:

- Use a distinct product identity and avoid implying that this modified build is endorsed by the original developer. The MIT text does not expressly grant trademark rights. A new name/icon is recommended; keep upstream credit in the notices or an about page.
- Prepare an accurate store listing describing the actual improvements: local capture, image waits, actual-position end detection, long-page sections and French/English menus.
- Publish an accurate privacy policy and complete Google's data disclosures. Google treats screenshots as handling user data and requires a privacy policy even when information stays on the device. Explain screenshot content, source page URLs/titles, local history, retention and deletion, and the absence of upload/sync services.
- Obtain a Chrome Web Store developer account and satisfy its registration, verification and submission requirements. MIT permission does not guarantee store approval.
- A store upload archive must have `manifest.json` at its root. The outer delivery ZIP includes source and instructions; create an upload ZIP from the contents of `extension/` when the public-release branding and listing are ready.

No store item was created or submitted as part of this update.
